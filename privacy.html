Privacy

This website collects nothing.

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels and no requests to anyone else. Here is exactly what happens when you visit, download or write to us — and how our software handles your data.

Last updated 11 October 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Delog (“Delog”, “we”, “us”). This policy covers the website and our contact with you. Each of our products also describes how it handles data — for Driftly, Tracemend and Prism, see “Our software” below.

What this website doesn’t do

  • No cookies. The site doesn’t set any.
  • No analytics or tracking. There are no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, fingerprinting, advertising tags or social-media widgets.
  • No third parties. Fonts, scripts, styles and images are all served from this site. While you read it, your browser talks to nobody else.
  • No accounts. No form that sends data. The contact form opens your own email app with your message filled in; contact links do the same.

What stays in your browser

If you choose Light or Dark in the theme switcher, your browser remembers the choice in its local storage under the name delog-theme. It never leaves your device. Choosing System in the footer removes it, as does clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Server logs

Like every website, this one is delivered by a web host — the company that hosts this website. Web hosts typically record standard request logs, such as your IP address, the time, the page or file requested and your browser’s user agent, to deliver the site, keep it secure and investigate abuse. We do not use these logs to identify, profile or market to visitors, and we do not combine them with other data. They are kept and deleted according to our hosting provider’s own retention policy.

Downloads

When you download an installer, your browser requests the file from the same host that serves this website, and the same kind of request log applies. We don’t ask who you are, and downloads aren’t linked to an identity. Checking the published SHA-256 checksum happens entirely on your computer.

Contact form

The contact form doesn’t send anything itself. It opens your own email app with your message filled in and addressed to us; nothing leaves your device until you send that email, which we then handle like any other email (see “Email” below).

Email

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to deal with your request — for example a support question or a security report. We keep correspondence for as long as we need it for that purpose and to meet our legal obligations, then delete it. We never sell or share it for marketing.

Our software

Driftly runs on your computer. It has no accounts, analytics or telemetry, and there is no Driftly server: snapshots, history, reports and settings are stored in a folder on your device, and passwords and tokens are encrypted with your operating-system account. Delog never receives this data.

Apart from visiting the websites you ask it to check, Driftly contacts other services only for features you switch on — such as Slack, Teams, email, webhook or Jira alerts, bug reports you file in Jira, GitHub, Linear or Azure DevOps, a cloud AI provider, Chrome UX Report data, domain-expiry lookups and update checks. Offline mode, which is on when you first install Driftly, blocks all of them. Data you send to those services is handled under their own privacy policies.

Tracemend also runs on your computer, with no accounts, analytics or telemetry and no Tracemend server. Projects, recordings, screenshots, run reports, traces, videos, network captures and settings are stored in a folder on your device; passwords, tokens and saved sign-ins are encrypted with your operating-system account. Crash reports stay on your device unless you turn on sending them and give an address. Delog never receives this data.

Apart from the websites and APIs you test, Tracemend contacts other services only for features you configure — an AI provider with your own API key (sent step kinds, locator descriptions and visible text, never screenshots or credentials), run results sent to Slack, email, webhooks, GitHub, Jira, TestRail, Xray or an OpenTelemetry collector, an update feed you set, and crash reports to an address you set. None of them is on until you set it up. Data you send to those services is handled under their own privacy policies.

Prism runs on your computer too, with no accounts, analytics, telemetry or update checks and no Prism server. Your workspaces are files you save where you choose; settings, backups and run history are stored in a folder on your device. The value of a secret variable is never written to a file, and an API key for the optional AI assistant is encrypted with your operating-system account.

Apart from the requests you send to the APIs you test, Prism contacts another service only for its AI assistant, which is off until you turn it on: it then sends the question you see before you ask it, with credentials and secret values masked, to Anthropic or an OpenAI-compatible service with your own API key — or to Ollama on your computer. Data you send to those services is handled under their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Because the website collects nothing, the only personal data we may hold about you is correspondence you sent us. You can ask us to access, correct or delete it, and you have the rights given to you by applicable law, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

The website and our software are made for professionals and are not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the website or our software handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
